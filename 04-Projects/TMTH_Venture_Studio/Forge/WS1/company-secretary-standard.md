**Owner:** Head of Product · **Capability:** Forge WS1 — CF development
**Version:** v0.1 · **Added:** 16 September 2026, under Tom's `/autonomous` instruction of the same day. Cause: "standard missing" reported at step 6b on Forge R2, R3, R5 and R6, and the seat named as owner of a build item at R1 with no standard to review it against.

## 1 · The frame — the seat that keeps the record of a legal person

Four facts fix the shape of this standard.

**1. The articles are the printed company's configuration baseline.** In systems engineering terms the model set of articles is the product baseline (ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 §6.3.5; SAE EIA-649C). Each printed company is one serial instance of that baseline with a short list of company-specific values: the name, the term date, the two sums, the ten call dates, the sponsor, the director. Every act the seat performs after incorporation is one of two things — a status-accounting event (a register entry, a filing) or a change to the baseline (a special resolution under s.21, sent to the registrar within 15 days under s.26 and s.30). The seat is the configuration manager of 148 legal persons at the count of record, and it must be able to say, for each one, which version of the model set it runs on.

**2. The seat records; the director acts; the operator decides.** Only a natural-person director can sign the accounts (s.414), make the solvency statement (s.643), apply for strike-off (s.1003) or open the bank account. Only the Portfolio Operator decides an exception or a term recommendation (`portfolio-operator-standard.md` PO-01, PO-38). The seat prepares the paper, keeps the registers, runs the calendar and delivers the filing. Where the record has the seat deciding or signing, the record is wrong about the seat.

**3. The design departs from the trade in six places, and each departure has paperwork.** A term written into the articles (R2). A ring-fence covenant, a class right on unspent budget and a call on the studio's stake (R3). Two classes, the sponsor's non-voting for the term, converting on a record entry rather than a resolution (R5). Partly paid shares in a private company, calls fixed in the articles, and a forfeiture automatic on the notice expiring (R6). A company incorporated to end. Each is lawful on the record's desk positions. Each changes what the seat files, records or must have accepted by a provider. The reviewer's job is to find the paper each one needs, not to mark the departure down.

**4. The hours are the test.** The record carries one company-secretariat FTE at head office (£75,000 loaded) at 148 live and per-company hours for only two acts: the conversion (one to three hours at the hold share) and the forfeiture entry (one hour at the default rate). Every other act — incorporation, the registrations, the allotment, the registers, the confirmation statement, the accounts delivery, the extension, the ordinary wind-down, a director change — is a driver with no hours. One FTE is an assertion until the sum of (driver × hours) reproduces it (EIA-748; the F2 rule of 4 September 2026).

**The conformity guard, applied to this seat.** The reviewer carries the trade's conventions by training. A board minute for every act. A directors' resolution to allot, to forfeit, to convert. A company secretary appointed in each company. Fully paid shares. Articles amended by a lawyer per company. An annual general meeting. None of these is a criterion. Reward a departure that is derived from the record: one model set, versioned; a forfeiture and a conversion that fire on a dated event and are recorded, not resolved; a sole director whose decisions are written records under model article 7. Fail only "cannot be executed by this role as specified". Two things can never be executed as specified: a filing with no natural person to sign it, and an allotment with no authority behind it.

## 3 · The shared rubric for Tier-2 checks

Tier-1 checks are mechanical: presence, count, arithmetic, traceability. Tier-2 checks are scored one to five by an independent scorer, never the producer; two scorers must agree; the pass mark is four.

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Stated in the component's definition, note or cost line, with a number, an owner and the section of law or the source relied on, and the reviewer can reproduce it from the record |
| 4 | As 5, but one figure is a band with no convergence event |
| 3 | Stated, with one of number, owner or source missing |
| 2 | Asserted in a sentence, with no field behind it |
| 1 | Absent |

These descriptors are the same as the sibling standards' and are drafted for ratification. The rubric is Tom's to ratify; applying it is permitted, changing it is not (README, autonomy boundaries).

## 6 · What a failed check becomes

A failed check is disposed under VA-89 and nothing else. The reviewer classifies; the reviewer does not fix.

| Failure | Class | What the record must carry |
|---|---|---|
| A provision, routine, register entry, filing, owner or figure is absent, or two documents disagree | **Design defect** | The check ID and the component; the challenge that must reopen |
| A figure exists as a band with no measurement yet | **Unmeasured input** (caps at PROVISIONAL) | Owner, convergence event, trigger and what the trigger moves |
| The thing exists in the design and is not yet built (the model set; the templates; the audit script) | **Build item** (caps at PROVISIONAL) | Owner, convergence event, re-run threshold |
| The failure is a limit of the world, true of every venture in the class | **Structural limit** (VA-101) | The residual, bounded, and the validation that addresses it |
| A standing ruling forbids the fix (no cash: no paid drafting, no authorised-person approval) | **Standing constraint** | The ruling cited |

An unclassifiable failed check is a design defect (VA-89's own residual rule).

1. **SEC-36 and SEC-33 — the ordinary wind-down has no secretarial hours.** The record prices one secretariat hour on a default (rate 0.02) and none on a wind-down at the term, which the kill band puts at 0.667 to 0.875 of terms. The strike-off application, the s.1006 copies, the s.644 filing, the closures and the register entries are the same acts either way. At this standard's estimate of ⚠ two hours, that is £67 to £88 a company-year at the band, on a line the record carries at £33. Design defect on the wind-down line.
2. **SEC-27 — "cancelled" needs a route.** The record says forfeited B shares are cancelled so that the studio's class is the whole. Section 662, which cancels forfeited shares within three years, applies only to public companies (s.662(1), read at source). In a private company the cancellation is a reduction of capital under ss.641 to 644 — the route the wind-down already runs to return unspent budget (FN-40). One reduction can do both. Design defect until the route is named. The same correction is made to FN-17 in `finance-standard.md`, which cited s.662 for a private company.
3. **SEC-14 and SEC-15 — no authority to allot, no exclusion of pre-emption.** With two classes s.550 does not apply; the B allotment needs a s.551 authority and the studio's pre-emption right under s.561 must be excluded, both in the articles. Neither appears in any of the four extensions of the build item. Design defect: the specification of the model set is incomplete.
4. **SEC-50 — the record names art. 28A; the offer as designed falls under art. 28.** Article 28A is the exemption for a one-off unsolicited real-time communication. The offer is made once, after the sponsor asks, on its own numbers — a solicited or non-real-time one-off, which is art. 28 (both headings read at source, 16 September 2026). The three one-off conditions are the same under either article, since art. 28A(3) applies art. 28(2) and (3) to decide what is one-off; the belt (art. 49) is unchanged; the trigger R5 carried ("part of an organised marketing campaign") is art. 28(3)(c). A citation defect in R5 §13, R6 §18.4 and the operating model's header, not a change to the design. Design defect (record).
5. **SEC-30 — the extension has no act and no share.** R3 names extend as an exit and R5 leaves the classes as they are for the new term. The act — a special resolution to change the term date, the B class's s.630 consent, a new allotment, a new calendar — is nowhere, and no share of terms is assigned to it. PO-16 asked for the rule; this is the paperwork and the driver. Design defect.
6. **SEC-11 — the registrations are not listed.** Corporation tax within three months of trading, the controller's fee (⚠ £52 a company-year at tier 1, £7,700 a year at 148 live — not in the floor), the VAT-group joining step. Small sums, but the F2 rule: a duty with no routine. Design defect.
7. **SEC-39 and SEC-41 — the confirmation statement and the accounts delivery are not the seat's acts in the record.** FN-33 asked finance for the statutory calendar; the seat delivers it. The £34 fee (£5,000 a year at 148 live) is not in the floor. Design defect on the floor; the hours go to SEC-53.
8. **SEC-53 — one FTE is asserted.** No act under the seat carries hours except the conversion and the forfeiture entry, so the FTE cannot be reproduced. On this standard's estimates (§4) the sum is about 7.5 to 8 hours a company-year, inside the 10.8 that one FTE gives at 148 live; at the R1 count of 1,130 it is 1.4 hours and fails. Nothing flips at the count of record; the derivation must exist. Design defect (arithmetic).
9. **SEC-46 — the director's other appointments.** A professional director holding 148 appointments, several in overlapping niches, is in a s.175 conflict at the second one unless the articles or the terms authorise it (s.180(4)(b)). The record never says so. Design defect on the model set's specification.
10. **SEC-31 — the call exit's paperwork.** The sponsor's call on the studio's A shares is a transfer: a stock transfer form, stamp duty at 0.5 per cent on the sponsor's side (⚠ £160 to £400 a company at a two-to-five-times trailing-revenue formula on the record's census median — this standard's estimate), registration on the stamped instrument. Absent from PP-1 and from the shareholders' business case. Design defect.
11. **SEC-09 — the subscriber sequence.** The studio incorporates alone and allots the B class afterwards; that is what makes the studio a subscriber rather than an agent filing on another's behalf, which the 2023 Act reserves to an authorised corporate service provider. The record says "at allotment" and never states the sequence. Design note; a one-line addition to WP-3.

## 8 · What this standard does not cover

- **Legal advice.** The record's desk positions — partly paid shares in a private company; the forfeiture as a proportionate class-right loss; the art. 28 and art. 49 readings; the printed company as neither a collective investment scheme nor a fund — are stated in the record with what a ruling would settle. This standard checks that they are stated and that the paper each needs exists. A ruling would settle only the dominant-influence reading under s.1162(2)(c), and that is finance's carried input, not this seat's.
- **The money.** The sums, the ring-fence's account structure, the return of capital's arithmetic, VAT, PAYE, the accounts' content. `finance-standard.md`. This standard files what finance computes.
- **The decisions.** Who decides an exception, the term, a stop. `portfolio-operator-standard.md`. This standard records what the operator decides.
- **The sponsor during the term.** The term report, the questions, the onboarding pack's content beyond the constitution and the calendar. `customer-success-standard.md`.
- **The design of the term, the classes, the schedule or the forfeiture.** That is R2's, R3's, R5's and R6's job. This standard reviews whether each design choice has its paper, not whether it is the right choice.
- **The professional director's own conduct** — the duties in ss.171 to 177 are the director's. This standard covers the appointment's paperwork, the terms the provider accepts, and the conflicts authorisation only.
- **The venture's own company** (the studio's confirmation statement, accounts and group accounts). Groups G and H apply to it as one more company; the group-accounts test is FN-35.
- **Scoring the Tier-2 rubric.** The descriptors in §3 are drafted for ratification and stay Tom's to change.
- **Jurisdictions other than England and Wales.**
