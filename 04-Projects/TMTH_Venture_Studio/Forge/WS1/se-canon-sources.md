# Systems engineering canon — the sources, the order, and the mapping to the CF

**Owner:** Head of Product (method owner: Head of R&D) · **Added:** 15 September 2026, on Tom's instruction.
**Rule:** Forge's core functionality is systems engineering applied to new-venture innovation. Before any process element is designed or changed, search here first. Then IVE. Then the management literature.

**Review date:** 15 December 2026 (90-day cycle; owner re-reads against the regression set and the canon mapping, bumps the date)

## 1 · Why the rule exists — the state on 15 September 2026

A count of the vault on the day the rule was written:

| Term | Files that use it |
|---|---|
| INCOSE | 144 |
| MBSE or SysML | 169 |
| ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 | 3 |
| SEBoK | 3 |
| NASA SE Handbook | 2 |
| MIL-STD-1521 or IEEE 15288.2 (technical reviews) | 4 |
| "specialty engineering" | 0 |
| "technical review" | 0 |
| "Design for X" | 0 |

The vault uses the vocabulary of systems engineering everywhere — V-model, PDR, CDR, TRR, NCR, TPM, first-article inspection — and consults its body of practice almost nowhere. The words were borrowed; the constructs behind them were not. A "CDR" with no entry and exit criteria is the failure. The role-review question of 15 September is the worked case: the CF had no slot for a specialist to review a function's planned activity, and the canon has had one for fifty years (specialty engineering, signed off at the technical review).

Both are dissertation material and both belong at the working group. **RD-027 (16 Sep 2026) ran the second pass: three of the four moved to Partial — tailored with citations; two genuine contributions remain — the reviewer-side conformity guard and the excluded-population rule. ## 4 · The check before any process change

1. Name the problem in one sentence.
2. Search SEBoK, then the NASA handbook, for the construct. Write down what the canon calls it.
3. If found: implement the construct, cite it, state the tailoring and why.
4. If not found: write "no SE analogue — venture-specific" on the element and add a row to section 3 marked **Finding**.
5. Then check IVE, then the management literature, for the content of the element.

Step 2 before step 5, always. The order is the rule.
