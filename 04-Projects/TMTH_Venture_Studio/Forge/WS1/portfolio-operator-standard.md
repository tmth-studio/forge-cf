# Portfolio Operator Standard — how one person oversees forty companies that have no people

**Owner:** Head of Product · **Capability:** Forge WS1 — CF development
**Version:** v0.2 · **Added:** 16 September 2026, under Tom's `/autonomous` instruction of the same day. Cause: "standard missing" reported at step 6b on Forge R1, R2, R3, R5 and R6 — the seat reviewed most often with no standard.
**Parent:** `derivation-standard.md` · **Siblings:** `customer-success-standard.md` (the sponsor during the term) · `finance-standard.md` (the money) · `tpm-measurement-standard.md` (how every figure is written)
**Applies to:** every operating-model component that carries `dri: Portfolio Operator`, and any function in the oversight pod that the seat performs — oversight, the term decision, the watchdog's escalations. Any name for the seat that reads the record and decides between the gates counts.
**Used at:** process flow v6 step 6b (the light pass per challenge; the full pass at Converge) and `realisation-standard.md` step 6b and step 9 (first-article inspection).

**What this is, and is not.** This is a **craft standard for an output the CF produces** — the oversight seat of an architected venture whose operating units are companies with no people. It is not a change to the CF method. It does not touch Simanis, the BALM disciplines or the criteria system. It gives the reviewing role its questions. The review's output is findings, never rewrites, disposed under VA-89 as design defect, standing constraint or build item. Jurisdiction for the statutory checks: England and Wales.

## 1 · The frame — the seat that runs the reviews between the gates

Four facts fix the shape of this standard.

**1. The seat is the human residue of the elimination, and it is sized in hours.** Forge's R1 removed the founding team. What remains on the human side is two seats: the Portfolio Operator at four hours a company-month, and the statutory director (R1 §9 and §14). Every hour the operator spends is a cost line in the floor — £200 a company-month at £50 an hour, £2,400 a company-year. So every routine the seat runs must be named, driven and timed, or the floor is wrong.

**2. The seat decides between the gates; it does not design.** In systems engineering terms the seat runs the technical reviews between the milestones. The monthly read is the interim assessment. The term gate is the review with entry and exit criteria (IEEE 15288.2; INCOSE SE Handbook 4th ed. ch. 10). The decisions it takes are pre-registered. The term is fixed in the constitution (R2, WP-5). The term budget is fixed at allotment and drawn by calls fixed in the articles (R3, R6). Attribution is classified by the agents at write time (R2). The seat reads and applies. Where the seat finds itself choosing, the rule is missing.

**3. Every decision rule is written before the first print, or the seat escalates commitment.** Decision-makers who have sunk resources continue to spend (Staw 1976). Two things reduce the effect: a pre-set limit, and separating the continue-decision from the party that made the first commitment (Sleesman et al. 2012, a 166-study meta-analysis). Institutional pressure produces the same escalation in professional investors who hold the information that should stop them (Guler 2007). The design's answer is structural. The term ends when the cash ends (R3). An extension is a new subscription. The hold and wind-down thresholds are numbers read from the record. This standard checks that those numbers exist before a company prints.

**4. One-off hours are deducted from capacity in the year they fall.** Datum R7 (4 September 2026) charged the one-off build as money and never deducted its hours from the 480-hour capacity; deducted, the binding gate failed by 23 per cent. The verification disposed of it as a design defect, not an unmeasured input. The same defect can sit in this seat's stage-1 figure. The rules, the escalation lines and the timing of the first cohort are one-off hours of the founder's 172.4. They are not in the 56.5 hours a print.

**The conformity guard, applied to this seat.** The reviewer carries the trade's conventions by training. A board seat per company. A monthly operating review held as a meeting. A dashboard of key indicators. A 100-day plan. An operating partner per company. Discretion to give a struggling company one more quarter. None of these is a criterion. Reward a departure that is derived from the record. One operator reads 40 records with no board seat and no meeting. The gate fires on cash, not on a committee. An extension exists only as fresh money on a fresh decision. Fail only "cannot be executed by this role as specified".

## 3 · The shared rubric for Tier-2 checks

Tier-1 checks are mechanical: presence, count, arithmetic, traceability. Tier-2 checks are scored one to five by an independent scorer, never the producer; two scorers must agree; the pass mark is four.

| Level | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | Stated in the component's definition, note or cost line, with a number, an owner and a driver, and the reviewer can reproduce it from the record |
| 4 | As 5, but one figure is a band with no convergence event |
| 3 | Stated, with one of number, owner or driver missing |
| 2 | Asserted in a sentence, with no field behind it |
| 1 | Absent |

These descriptors are the same as the sibling standards' and are drafted for ratification. The rubric is Tom's to ratify; applying it is permitted, changing it is not (README, autonomy boundaries).

## 6 · What a failed check becomes

A failed check is disposed under VA-89 and nothing else. The reviewer classifies; the reviewer does not fix.

| Failure | Class | What the record must carry |
|---|---|---|
| A rule, routine, threshold, owner or figure is absent, or two figures disagree | **Design defect** | The check ID and the component; the challenge that must reopen |
| A figure exists as a band with no measurement yet | **Unmeasured input** (caps at PROVISIONAL) | Owner, convergence event, trigger and what the trigger moves |
| The thing exists in the design and is not yet built | **Build item** (caps at PROVISIONAL) | Owner, convergence event, re-run threshold |
| The failure is a limit of the world, true of every venture in the class | **Structural limit** (VA-101) | The residual, bounded, and the validation that addresses it |
| A standing ruling forbids the fix | **Standing constraint** | The ruling cited |

An unclassifiable failed check is a design defect (VA-89's own residual rule).

Nothing flips; the arithmetic must be one number. Design defect (arithmetic). *(DA-12 answered — Head of R&D, 21 September 2026, object-after 23 September 17:00. ## 8 · What this standard does not cover

- **The sponsor during the term** — the term report, the questions, the term meeting. `customer-success-standard.md`.
- **The money** — the calls, the ring-fence, the return of capital, the settlement. `finance-standard.md`. This standard sequences those routines; it does not review their content.
- **The design of the term, the thresholds or the attribution classes.** That is R2's, R3's and R6's job. This standard reviews whether the rules exist and can be applied by this seat, not whether they are the right rules.
- **The professional director's own conduct** — the duties in ss.171 to 177 are the director's, and the operator cannot hold or discharge them. This standard covers the appointment's maintenance and the director's priced acts only.
- **The watchdog service's internal design** — the open-job shape and the stall threshold are the Head of Method's (NCR-C21) and `customer-success-standard.md` CS-20 to CS-23.
- **Frontier-programme economics** — the kill band, the payback, the four conditions. R3 §8 and the Studio Director's capital rule. PO-21 checks only that the calibration routine exists.
- **Scoring the Tier-2 rubric.** The descriptors in §3 are drafted for ratification and stay Tom's to change.
- **Jurisdictions other than England and Wales** for the statutory checks in groups F and G.
