FORGE · FREE

Design a venture-scale company from one prompt

Describe an idea, or name a business you want to beat. Forge designs the company that could reach scale, tests it at ten gates and gives you a business case and a live financial workbook.

1

Open an agent that can work on files

Any one of these. You do not need to use a terminal.

Claude Code (desktop app) Codex Cursor GitHub Copilot, agent mode
⚠ A plain chat window such as claude.ai or ChatGPT will not work. Forge writes files and runs checks, so it needs an agent that can do both.
2

Copy this and paste it into the agent

Set up Forge by following https://github.com/tmth-studio/forge-cf/blob/main/AGENTS.md and help me start my first venture.

The agent puts Forge in your Documents folder, installs what it needs, checks the setup worked and starts. Then it asks for your idea — a sentence is enough, or the name of a business you want to beat. It asks before it installs anything on the computer.

What you get at the end

  • a business case document in the seven sections of the GOV.UK business plan template
  • an Excel workbook with live formulas: sales, 12-month cash flow and five years of forecast statements
  • the design behind them: ten requirements, each with a pass or fail, and a score for the whole

A design that fails a gate stops there and tells you why. That is a result, not an error.

What it costs

The method is free. You pay only for your own agent’s usage.
